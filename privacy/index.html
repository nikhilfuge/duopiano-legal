Duo Piano Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · App: Duo Piano for iPhone · Developer: Neel Studios

This policy explains what information the Duo Piano app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: Duo Piano has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. Your practice data and your own music files stay on your device. Information leaves your device only for subscription purchases (handled by Apple and our subscription provider, RevenueCat) and for anonymous usage statistics (handled by TelemetryDeck), which you can turn off in the app.

1. Information stored only on your device

The app saves the following on your iPhone so it can remember your preferences. We cannot see it, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Settings such as note names, fingering, key labels, keyboard size, Learn Assist and volume.
  • Your favourite songs and your recently played songs.
  • Your learning progress: practice time, sessions, completed songs, streaks, achievements and where you stopped in a song.
  • MIDI files you choose to import, and the songs made from them. Imported files are read on your device and never uploaded.

This data is deleted when you delete the app. If your device backs up to iCloud or a computer, it may be included in that backup under Apple’s terms.

All music is played by a sound engine built into the app. The app does not use the microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location, and the song library works without an internet connection.

2. Subscription purchases

Duo Piano offers an optional Premium subscription. When you view plans, subscribe or restore purchases:

  • Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, email address, Apple Account details or payment card information. Apple’s handling of your data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. provides our subscription infrastructure. It checks App Store transactions and tells the app whether Premium is active. To do this, RevenueCat receives the information below.
InformationPurpose
A random, anonymous app user ID created by the RevenueCat software (not linked to your name or Apple Account) To associate your subscription with your device and restore it
App Store transaction and purchase information: products purchased, purchase and expiry dates, renewal status To validate purchases and unlock Premium
Technical information sent with network requests: app version, device model, operating system version, App Store country, and IP address To operate and secure the service and show prices for your country

We use this information only to provide and manage your subscription. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or combine it with data from other apps or websites.

3. Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which features help people learn, and to improve the app, Duo Piano sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany). These cover, for example, that a song was started or completed, that a practice session ended, that the subscription screen was opened, or that a purchase succeeded. They include:

  • The event and simple details about it, such as the name of a built-in song, its level, the practice feature used, or a practice-time range (for example “5–15 minutes”).
  • A device identifier that is one-way hashed on your device before it is sent, so it cannot be traced back to you or your device. It is used only to count users and sessions.
  • General technical details: app version, operating system version, device model, language and region.

Usage statistics never include your name, email address, Apple Account, payment details, the notes you play, or anything about the MIDI files you import (imported songs are reported only as “imported”). They are not linked to your identity, not combined with data from other apps or websites, and not used for advertising or tracking. TelemetryDeck processes them on our behalf under its own privacy policy.

Your choice: you can turn usage statistics off at any time in the app under Settings › Privacy & Legal › Share Anonymous Usage Data. The app works the same either way.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require or offer user accounts or sign-in.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • We do not use analytics services other than the anonymous usage statistics described in section 3, and we do not use crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

5. Data retention and deletion

Information stored on your device stays there until you delete the app. RevenueCat keeps subscription records for as long as needed to provide the subscription and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. To ask for subscription records associated with your app user ID to be deleted, contact us (see section 10). Deleting these records does not cancel an active subscription. Anonymous usage statistics are kept in aggregated form to show trends; because they are not linked to you, we cannot pick out or delete one person’s statistics, but you can stop them being sent by turning off Share Anonymous Usage Data. Cancel in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, India or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the app has no accounts, the only personal information we can act on is subscription data held by RevenueCat. Contact us and we will help, including by working with RevenueCat. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

7. Children

Duo Piano is suitable for learners of all ages. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children: it has no accounts, chat or social features, practice data never leaves the device, and usage statistics are anonymous. Purchases are made through Apple, whose parental controls (such as Ask to Buy) apply. If you believe a child’s information has been collected through a purchase, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security and international transfers

Communication with Apple, RevenueCat and TelemetryDeck is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries where it or its service providers operate, with safeguards described in its privacy policy. TelemetryDeck processes usage statistics in the European Union.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of information changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a change is significant, we will also say so in the app or in the App Store release notes.
